# LinkedIn: A one-page AI risk briefing for your board

**Post on:** Nov 17, 2026
**Blog post:** https://courtneyroot.dev/writing/one-page-ai-risk-board-briefing/

## Post

Boards keep asking "what's our AI risk?" I think most answers miss what a board actually needs.

A board doesn't need to understand prompt injection. It needs to decide how much risk the company accepts and fund the work to stay inside that line.

So I made a free one-page template for an AI risk briefing. It covers:
• Where the company uses AI, and how much of it can act without a person approving
• The top three risks, written as things that happen to the business
• What's being done, with owners and dates
• The decisions the board needs to make
• Four trend measures, quarter over quarter

The section most updates leave out is the decisions. If the board leaves without making one, the risk got accepted by default.
