# LinkedIn: The $50 break-in

**Post on:** Oct 20, 2026
**Blog post:** https://courtneyroot.dev/writing/ai-agent-hacking-spree-50-dollar-break-in/

## Post

An attacker reportedly pointed AI agents at about 1,200 websites and got into more than 100 companies for around $5,000 in AI usage. That's roughly $50 per break-in.

What stood out to me wasn't the AI. In one case, the agents found nothing wrong with the main site, so they went looking at related infrastructure and found a control panel nobody was watching. That's an old playbook. What changed is the cost of running it.

For a long time, a lot of companies were protected by not being worth a full day of an attacker's time. I don't think that's true anymore.

A few things I'd look at this week:
• Every internet-facing system you have, especially admin panels and sites vendors run for you
• Admin interfaces that are reachable from the open internet
• How long it takes to patch public-facing systems
