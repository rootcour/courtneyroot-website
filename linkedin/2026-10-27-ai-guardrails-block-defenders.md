# LinkedIn: When AI guardrails block the defenders

**Post on:** Oct 27, 2026
**Blog post:** https://courtneyroot.dev/writing/ai-guardrails-block-defenders/

## Post

Twice this year, a security team in the middle of an incident asked an AI model for help and got refused.

Hugging Face's responders couldn't get commercial models to analyze their forensic logs in July. This month, OpenCode's team had models stop helping once the malicious code they were studying tripped safety filters.

I don't think the guardrails are wrong. A model can't tell a defender from an attacker. But defenders hit that wall at the worst possible time.

What I'd set up before you need it:
• Test your AI-assisted incident response with realistic samples now
• Enroll in your AI providers' vetted programs for security teams ahead of time
• Have a backup model you can run on your own infrastructure
• Decide what incident data is allowed to go to a third-party AI at all

Someone has to decide what AI will do for your defenders, not just what it won't do for attackers. More in the comments.
