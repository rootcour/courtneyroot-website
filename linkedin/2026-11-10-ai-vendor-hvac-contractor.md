# LinkedIn: Your AI vendor is the new HVAC contractor

**Post on:** Nov 10, 2026
**Blog post:** https://courtneyroot.dev/writing/target-breach-revisited-ai-vendors/

## Post

In grad school I wrote about the 2013 Target breach. Attackers got in through a refrigeration contractor's login and worked their way to the registers.

I reread it recently. The lesson still holds: your security includes everyone you've given a key to. But the keys look different now.

In 2013, vendor access meant a login on your network. Today it's usually an OAuth token or API key that lets a vendor's software act inside your SaaS systems directly. In 2025, attackers stole tokens from the Drift AI chat agent and used them to pull data from companies' Salesforce instances. Network segmentation doesn't help when there's no network hop.

What I'd do now:
• Inventory every AI tool connected to your systems
• Cut their access down to what they actually need
• Practice revoking a vendor's access in minutes
